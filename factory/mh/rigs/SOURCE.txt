Official MakeHuman default rig for hm08 basemesh (CC0).

Source: makehumancommunity/makehuman makehuman/data/rigs/
  default.mhskel — bone hierarchy + joint vertex indices + roll planes
  default_weights.mhw — per-vertex bone weights on basemesh
