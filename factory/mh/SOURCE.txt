Poses — read factory/mh/POSES.md (verified against makehuman/plugins):

  Face in MH app: face-poseunits.json + face-poseunits.bvh + framemapping.

  body-poseunits.json: no BVH, not loaded by MH core; not a standalone web runtime format.

  Official mesh pose: skeleton.setPose + skeleton.skinMesh with default.mhskel + default_weights.mhw.

  Web ships now: factory/pack_poses.py → official-targets-v1 (arms/legs modeling .target @ 1.0).

  pack_skeleton.py / body-rig.js kept for future MH Collada/glTF or skinMesh bake only.

What models/base.obj alone does NOT include:
  armature, inverse bind matrices, skin weights, posed vertices.
  joint-* groups in the OBJ are helper cubes for DCC tools, not a runtime rig.
