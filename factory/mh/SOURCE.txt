Official CC0 MakeHuman hm08 targets. Used only to know which body
vertices are lips and cheeks — not a geometric guess.

Source: makehumancommunity/makehuman (CC0, September 2020)
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-upperlip-volume-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-lowerlip-volume-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-lowerlip-width-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-lowerlip-ext-up.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-angles-up.target (high displacement only)
  makehuman/data/targets/cheek/l-cheek-volume-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/cheek/r-cheek-volume-incr.target

Volume is the lip pads. lowerlip-width + lowerlip-ext are the outer lip.
mouth-angles-up at high displacement is the official commissure; the
low-mag falloff of that target walks onto the cheek and is dropped.

Official default.mhmat sets shaderConfig vertexColors false.
The official skin shader colours the body with texture2D on the
hm08 UV, not per-vertex colours. Makeup in this repo paints those
official UV islands.

Poses (factory/pack_poses.py):
  Official MH *poses* need skeleton + makehuman/data/poseunits/body-poseunits.json
  + skin weights (see makehuman animation3d). hm08 web export has no armature;
  we do not rotate verts around joints (breaks normals).
