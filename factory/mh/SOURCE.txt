Official CC0 MakeHuman hm08 data used by the factory (not guessed geometry).

Source repo: makehumancommunity/makehuman (CC0, September 2020)

Makeup zones (factory/pack_makeup_zones.py):
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-upperlip-volume-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-lowerlip-volume-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-lowerlip-width-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-lowerlip-ext-up.target
  makehuman/data/targets/mouth/mouth-angles-up.target (high displacement only)
  makehuman/data/targets/cheek/l-cheek-volume-incr.target
  makehuman/data/targets/cheek/r-cheek-volume-incr.target

Body morphs: vendored under factory/mh/targets/ (see factory/models/SOURCE.txt).

Poses (official pipeline):
  factory/mh/rigs/default.mhskel + default_weights.mhw — skeleton + skin weights
  factory/mh/poseunits/body-poseunits.json — pose unit bone quaternions
  factory/pack_skeleton.py → web/data/body-skeleton.json
  factory/pack_poses.py → web/data/body-poses.json (unit recipes only)
  Runtime: web/body-rig.js (SkinnedMesh + pose units)

What models/base.obj alone does NOT include:
  armature, inverse bind matrices, skin weights, posed vertices.
  joint-* groups in the OBJ are helper cubes for DCC tools, not a runtime rig.
